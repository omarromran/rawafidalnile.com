## Approval rules

- English and Arabic must describe the same fact and scope.
- Names, titles, addresses, numbers, and legal terms must be checked against the approved corporate profile.
- Claims about projects, subsidiaries, partnerships, licenses, registrations, or formation status require owner approval.
- Do not publish private contact details or partner information unless explicitly approved.
- Record the approver and approval date in the change or commit message.
